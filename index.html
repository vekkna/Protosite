Playing a battle
  • Tap an available unit on the battlefield. If there’s a terrain tile, drag it onto the unit and adjust it there. Drag the unit into your deployment zone to lock the terrain. Tap or drag the unit’s ↻ handle to rotate. Undo any adjustment before choosing Confirm Unit Deployment.
  • Choose a command bid and lock it when ready. Both bids are revealed together. The lower bidder acts first and last.
  • Your highlighted units can be commanded. A unit's first order spends a command.
  • Drag along a unit’s movement outline to preview its destination. Release to move. A red outline marks a charge.
  • Select a unit and drag the ↻ handle behind it to pivot; drag the unit itself to move. Choose Reform first to spend the action on a turn of any size allowed by the surrounding space.
  • While dragging a ranged unit, its firing arc follows the preview. Gold outlines show clear sightlines. Dashed outlines show blocked targets. Click a blocked target to see the reason.
  • Select an outlined enemy to see the odds. Select it again to shoot or strike.
  • With the battlefield focused, use Up/Down to move and Left/Right to turn. Hold Shift for smaller steps. Use Enter or Space on a focused enemy to target it.
  • Enter ends the activation. Escape clears a selected order or target.
  • Right-drag or middle-drag anywhere to measure. On touch, tap Ruler then drag. Keep dragging past an edge to release the snap, or hold Alt for free placement.
  • Pin up to two rulers. Use Pin to units to keep snapped endpoints attached as units move. If a unit routs, its endpoint stays at the last position. Shift-click units to compare ranges.
  • Click Ping or press P, then tap a spot on the battlefield to mark it for both players.
  • Press Z to undo steps until a roll, reveal or phase change. Online, undo is available during your command window. Press F for full screen.
