    Field Controls

    Mouse and Keyboard
    • Choose New Rules Game; the guide above the battlefield always shows the next choice.
    • Click highlighted units and targets. Drag highlighted terrain or the active unit when prompted.
    • Use a piece's rotation handle for a legal pivot, then choose its action in the guide.
    • Drag on the battlefield to measure distance.
    • To play remotely, host the game, share the code, and let Player 2 join. The host confirms every rules action.
    • Sandbox / Quick Deploy restores free movement, manual wounds, dice, rotation shortcuts, and undo.
    • F toggles full screen.
    Touch
    • Play in landscape. The guide stays above the battlefield and can be collapsed for more room.
    • Tap highlighted units or targets; drag terrain and moving units with one finger when prompted.
    • Drag the circular rotation handle to spend one legal pivot.
    • Drag empty battlefield space to measure distance.
    • Sandbox / Quick Deploy restores unrestricted piece controls.
