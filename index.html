Field Controls

Mouse and Keyboard
  • Lock your bid, and reveal when both players are ready.
  • Drag command circles onto units to activate them.
  • Pass to advance the turn between Master Tactician and Player X.
  • Drag a unit or terrain to move it.
  • Drag on the battlefield to measure distance.
  • Drag the circle around a selected piece to rotate it.
  • Shift-click units to compare their ranges. Click empty battlefield space or press Escape to clear.
  • +/- over a unit changes strain.
  • Z or Ctrl+Z undoes the last change.
  • F toggles full screen.
Touch
  • Drag units or terrain with one finger.
  • Long-press a unit to select multiple ranges, then tap units to add or remove them. Tap empty battlefield space to clear.
  • Drag command circles onto units.
  • Select a piece to show movement controls.
  • Use Pass to advance the round’s turns.
  • Drag the circle around a selected piece to rotate; use +/- to adjust strain.
  • Drag empty battlefield space to measure distance.
